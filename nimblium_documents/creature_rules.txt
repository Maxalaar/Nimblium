# Creature Rules — Nimblium

**House rule.** Nimblium departs from Nimble on how creatures act. In Nimble, a monster does
one thing per turn, and its card links its attacks with "OR" (pick one), "Then" (chain them)
and "(2×)". Nimblium gives creatures **actions**, like heroes, and writes their abilities with
the same blocks and capsules as hero abilities. One card language for the whole game.

Do not "fix" these rules from the Nimble books or changelog.

## 1. Actions

- **Each creature has 1 action per turn.** This is the default value: it is not written on the
  card (like speed 6).
- **Stronger creatures have more actions.** Their number is written as the `ACT` stat at the
  bottom of the card, only when it is more than 1.
- **Moving is free.** A creature moves up to its speed on its turn without spending an action,
  as in Nimble. Only heroes spend actions to move.
- **Each active ability costs actions**, shown on a capsule (`1 Action`). A creature can use
  any active ability it can pay for.
- **Limits prevent abuse.** A creature with 2 actions must not be able to use its strongest
  attack twice: give that ability a `1/turn` limit.
- **Legendary creatures** have "Legendary" in their types. They still take a turn after each
  hero's turn (Nimble rule): this is a game rule and is not written on the card.
  They have their `ACT` actions on each of these turns. Limits such as `1/round` keep them in
  check.
- **Saves, Bloodied, Last Stand.** A legendary creature's Nimble sections become passive
  blocks, written in full on its own card (there is no shared Legendary card):
  - **Saves**: "This creature has advantage on STR and DEX saves."
  - **Bloodied**: "While this creature is Bloodied, its attacks deal 2d8+3 damage instead."
    (+ Bloodied reminder)
  - **Last Stand**: "When this creature drops to 0 HP, it dies only after taking 20 more
    damage. Until then, its Defense is 20."
- **Summoned companions** controlled by a hero (Spirit Companion) have no actions of their
  own: a passive block says so, and their controller pays the costs on their capsules.
- **Conditions apply as written.** Dazed takes 1 action away from the creature's next turn:
  a creature with 1 action does nothing that turn except move. Dying limits it to 1 action.

## 2. Blocks

A creature's abilities are a list of blocks, exactly like a hero ability card:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `name` | the ability's name, shown in bold above the capsules |
| `kind` | `"passive"` (always on or triggered) or `"active"` (costs actions) |
| `cost` | `(actions(1),)`: every active block has a cost, even when it is 1 action |
| `limitation` | `per_turn(1)`, `per_round(1)`, `per_encounter(1)`... |
| `attacks` | number of attacks, when more than 1: `2` → capsule `2 attacks` |
| `reach` | melee reach, when more than 1: `2` → capsule `Reach 2` |
| `range` | ranged attacks only: `8` → capsule `Range 8` |
| `damage` | the attack's damage: `"1d6+2"` → capsule `1d6+2 damage` (content such as `[Die+#WIL radiant]` also works) |
| `effect` | the text, optional: a plain attack is fully described by its capsules |

A block with a `damage` field is an attack. Its capsules come in this order: mode, cost,
limit, attacks, reach or range, damage.

Passive blocks come first, then active blocks: the template sorts them that way.

**Long creatures.** One file per creature, always, and one card by default: a text that is too
long overflows visibly. To allow two cards, add `split: true` to the creature: if its text
doesn't fit on one card even at the smallest text size, it is compiled into two cards numbered
"1/2" and "2/2" in their types. The first one has the stats and as many blocks as fit, the
second one the remaining blocks and the reminders. Use it only when the text can't be shortened
(legendary creatures).

## 3. Text

- **Every block has a name**, in its `name` field: it is shown in bold above the capsules and
  lets the GM and the players refer to the ability. Hero ability blocks have no name; the
  field is the same, it is just left out.
- **Attacks go on capsules, not in the text.** The number of attacks, the reach or range and
  the damage are the `attacks`, `reach`, `range` and `damage` fields (§2). A melee attack with
  Reach 1 has neither reach nor range: it is the default. `attacks: 2` replaces Nimble's "(2×)".
- **Attack riders** are full sentences with "this attack" as subject:
  - On damage: "Whenever this attack deals damage, the target is Dazed."
  - On crit: "Whenever this attack crits, the target is Prone."
  - Movement before the attack: "It may move up to 6 spaces before this attack."
  - Escape: "the target is Grappled until it escapes with a DC 10 STR or DEX save."
- **Damage that is not an attack** (a charge through a line, a creature swung at another)
  stays in the text: "Each creature in its path takes 2d6 damage."
- **Summons**: "it summons a Goblin Minion adjacent to it". A minion's die size is its damage
  die: Nimble's "10 scarab minions (d6)" becomes "It summons 10 scarab minions within 6
  spaces. Each one deals 1d6 damage."
- **Subject.** A creature is "this creature", then "it / its". Never "you": "you" is the player
  who owns the card (writing_rules.txt §2).
- **No "or", no "then".** Two attacks the creature chooses between are two blocks. Two effects
  it chains are two blocks, each with a `1/turn` limit, on a creature with enough actions.
- **No costs or limits in the text.** They go on capsules (writing_rules.txt §4).
- **Default values are not written**: speed 6, 1 action, Medium size, can take damage, disappears at 0 HP.

## 4. Stats

The stat row at the bottom of the card shows, in this order and only when present:

`ACT` (when more than 1) · `HP` · `DEF` · `SPD` (when not 6) · `SIZE` (when not Medium) · `LVL`

- `DEF` (field `defense`) is the damage the creature ignores from each attack: Nimble's Medium
  armor is DEF 10, Heavy armor is DEF 20. No `DEF` means 0. Crits ignore it.
- Minions have no `HP` and no `LVL`.
- `SIZE` (field `size`: "Tiny", "Small", "Large", "Huge", "Gargantuan") comes from the
  source stat block. Size governs forced movement and moving through other creatures' spaces
  (2026-10-02 changelog). Medium is the default and is not written.

## 5. Shared blocks

`creatures/passive_abilities.typ` holds the blocks several creatures share. Use them instead of
rewriting the text:

| Block | Who | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `passive_abilities.undying` | most Undead | `1/encounter`: when it would die, it resets to 1 HP |
| `passive_abilities.flying` | creatures that fly (Nimble's "Fly") | "This creature is Flying." Add `keywords.flying` to its reminders |
| `passive_abilities.minion` | every minion (type "Minion") | any damage kills it; its attacks can't crit and ignore advantage and disadvantage; several minions attacking the same creature count as a single attack |

## 6. Converting a Nimble stat block

| Nimble | Nimblium |
|---|---|
| `Stab. 1d6. OR: Shoot. (Range 8) 1d6.` | two active blocks, `1 Action` each |
| `Swarm. ... Then: Slam (2×). 1d4+20.` | `ACT 2`, two active blocks, `1 Action` and `1/turn` each |
| `Slam (2×). 1d4+8.` | one block named Slam: `attacks: 2, damage: "1d4+8"` |
| `Shoot. (Range 8) 1d6.` | `range: 8, damage: "1d6"` |
| `On damage: Dazed.` / `On crit: Prone.` | "Whenever this attack deals damage, the target is Dazed." / "Whenever this attack crits, ..." |
| "The first time this dies, ..." | passive block with a `1/encounter` limit (Undying is shared: `passive_abilities.undying` in `creatures/passive_abilities.typ`) |
| `Medium` / `Heavy` armor | `defense: 10` / `defense: 20` |
| `Lvl 1/4` minion | no level, type "Minion", `passive_abilities.minion` |
| Bloodied / Last Stand abilities | passive blocks whose text starts with "While this creature is Bloodied, ..." / "When this creature drops to 0 HP, ..." |

## 7. Examples

**Goblin** — 1 action (not written): it stabs or shoots.

```typst
blocks: (
  (kind: "active", cost: (actions(1),), name: "Stab", damage: "1d6+2"),
  (kind: "active", cost: (actions(1),), name: "Shoot", range: 8, damage: "1d6+2"),
),
hit_points: 15,
level: "1/2",
```

**Goblin Ratrider** — a passive and one attack.

```typst
blocks: (
  (kind: "passive", name: "Charge", effect: [If this creature moves at least 4 spaces in a straight line, its next attack this turn has advantage.]),
  (kind: "active", cost: (actions(1),), name: "Bite & Stab", attacks: 2, damage: "1d6+2", effect: [Whenever this attack crits, the target is #kw_prone.]),
),
reminder: (conditions.prone,),
hit_points: 30,
speed: 10,
level: "2",
```

**Mummy Lord** — 2 actions: it summons and slams each turn, but never slams twice.

```typst
blocks: (
  passive_abilities.undying,
  (kind: "passive", name: "Cursed Gaze", effect: [Whenever a creature crits this creature, that creature makes a #dc(20) #INT save. If it fails, it gains 1 Wound.]),
  (kind: "active", cost: (actions(1),), limitation: per_turn(1), name: "Scarab Swarm", effect: [It summons 10 scarab minions within 6 spaces. Each one deals 1d6 damage.]),
  (kind: "active", cost: (actions(1),), limitation: per_turn(1), name: "Slam", attacks: 2, damage: "1d4+20", effect: [Whenever this attack deals damage, the target is #kw_dazed.]),
),
reminder: (conditions.dazed,),
hit_points: 280,
actions: 2,
level: "21",
```
