## Set aside

Features deliberately left out of the game for now. Nothing in the cards or class sheets
refers to them.

- **Beastmaster (Hunter): needs a deep rework** (decided 2026-11-26). Not
  implemented on purpose. The book's version (an abstract companion with no stats, Small,
  Medium or Large, whose abilities replace 2 Thrill of the Hunt abilities) doesn't fit how
  Nimblium wants companions: they must be creatures with HP and a position (like the Shepherd's
  Spirit Companion and the Songweaver's Friends). A first creature version was tried and
  dropped: Protect Me! making an attack miss outright made no sense, the Small Beast was much
  weaker than the others, and the cards didn't fit. Goal for the rework: any hero, of any
  class, could take a beast companion instead of their subclass, with its own progression
  tree. To discuss before writing any card.
- **Boons** (decided 2026-11-28): removed from the game, too much balancing for now. The book's
  Minor, Major and Epic Boons (Game Master's Guide p.23) have no cards, and the class sheets no
  longer show the level 19 Epic Boon (that level gives nothing). A first idea, kept for later:
  Epic Boons should be stronger than an ancestry trait at level 19 (an effect every round, a
  value that grows with LVL or KEY, or a radical effect), then the level 20 capstones checked
  against them.
