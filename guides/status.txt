# Project status — Nimblium

Where each class, spell school and creature family stands. To see which cards are up to date
with the style and the changelog: `python3 tools/review.py`. For deliberate departures from
the game: `python3 tools/review.py --rules`.

The changelogs announce new subclasses for almost every class, but only give their names: no
text is available, so no cards.

## Classes

- **Berserker** (p.6-11): COMPLETE — base levels, Savage Arsenal (Dismantle included),
  Mountainheart and Red Mist subclasses. In the new style and up to the October 2, 2026
  changelog. Announced subclasses: Path of Burning Rage, Path of Titan's Grip, Muscle Mage.
- **Cheat** (p.12-17): COMPLETE — base levels, Underhanded Abilities (Tangling Wire and The
  Setup included), Scoundrel and Silent Blade subclasses. In the new style and up to the
  October 2, 2026 changelog. Announced subclasses: Tools of the Gambler, Tools of the Serpent,
  Honorseeker.
- **Hunter** (p.24-29): COMPLETE — base levels, Thrill of the Hunt, Shadowpath (Dread Hunter
  replaces Pack Hunter) and Wild Heart subclasses. Uses `hunter_keywords.typ` for Marked
  (replaces the PDF's "quarry" prose). In the new style and up to the October 2, 2026
  changelog. Announced subclasses: Keeper of Traps, Keeper of the Pack.
- **Mage** (p.30-35): COMPLETE — base abilities, Spellshaper (with a Mechanic card for the
  limit of 1 Spellshaper per turn), Invoker of Control and Invoker of Chaos subclasses. In the
  new style and up to the October 2, 2026 changelog. `mage_keywords.typ` defines Demand Control
  and Invoke Chaos. No card for At Any Cost or Tempest Mage (they only unlock spells), nor for
  the Chaos Table (secret, GM only, remade in the changelog without its content being
  published). Announced subclasses: Invoker of Wards, Flame, Surges, Frost.
- **Shepherd** (p.48-53): COMPLETE — base levels, Sacred Graces, Luminary of Mercy and
  Luminary of Malice subclasses. In the new style. The companion stays in its house version:
  the October 2, 2026 changelog changes to the companion are not applied, except two (heals
  limited to WIL/Safe Rest, Vengeful Spirit as a single burst), and we balance the Shepherd
  ourselves (note in `game/class/shepherd/mod.typ`). Announced subclasses: Luminary of
  Protection, Luminary of the Forge.
- **Shadowmancer** (p.42-47): COMPLETE — reserved cantrips (Shadow Blast, Summon Shadow,
  Command Shadows), Shadow and Greater Shadow creatures, Pilfered Power as a Mechanic card,
  Greedy Pact, Dire Shadows, Eldritch Usurper, Lesser and Greater Invocations (Vengeful Blast
  removed by the changelog), Pact of the Red Dragon and Pact of the Abyssal Depths subclasses.
  In the new style and up to the October 2, 2026 changelog. No card for Master of Darkness or
  Shadowmastery (they only unlock spells). The Reaver waits with the Extra Subclasses. Class
  icon: Death Note. Announced subclasses: Pact of the Void, Pact of the Endless Swarm, Pact
  of the High Celestial.
- **Commander** (p.18-23): COMPLETE — Coordinated Strike!, Combat Dice (Mechanic card), Field
  Medic, Master Commander, Weapon Mastery, Unparalleled Tactics, Captain of Legions;
  Commander's Orders (Commanding Presence moves there, from the changelog), Combat Tactics,
  Weapon Masteries (remade), Champion of the Bulwark and Champion of the Vanguard subclasses;
  class sheet. In the new style and up to the October 2, 2026 changelog. The Spellblade waits
  with the Extra Subclasses. Class icon: Trumpet Flag. Announced subclasses: Champion of the
  Arena, of the Phalanx, of the Siege-Breaker.
- **Oathsworn** (p.36-41): COMPLETE — Radiant Judgment, Lay on Hands, Oathsworn Mana Pool
  (Mechanic card, formula only), Zealot, Paragon of Virtue, My Life for My Friends, Unending
  Judgment, Glorious Paragon; Sacred Decrees; Oath of Vengeance and Oath of Refuge
  subclasses; class sheet. In the new style and up to the October 2, 2026 changelog. The
  Oathbreaker waits with the Extra Subclasses. Class icon: Winged Shield. Announced
  subclasses: Oath of Roaring Thunder, Oath of Valor.
- **Songweaver** (p.54-59): COMPLETE — Vicious Mockery (Songweaver-only cantrip),
  Songweaver's Inspiration, Songweaver Mana Pool, Song of Rest, Quick Wit, I'm So Famous!;
  Lyrical Weaponry; the 4 Friends (A "People" Person), creature cards that follow the
  Songweaver; Herald of Snark and Herald of Courage
  subclasses; class sheet. Up to the October 2, 2026 changelog. No card for Jack of All Trades
  and Perform! (house rule) nor for I Know Just the Song (only unlocks spells). Class icon:
  Lyre. Announced subclasses: Herald of Singing Steel, Herald of Torment, Herald of Legends.
- **Stormshifter** (p.60-65): COMPLETE — rebuilt by the October 2, 2026 changelog: Beastshift
  (free harmless forms, Direbeast forms DEX/encounter), Stormshifter Mana Pool, Stormborn,
  Archdruid; the 3 Direbeast forms (Fearsome Beast, Beast of the Pack, Beast of Nightmares) as
  ability cards; 9 Chimeric Boons; Circle of Sky & Storm and Circle of Fang & Claw; class
  sheet. stormshifter_keywords.typ defines Direbeast Form. No card for Be Wild (house rule), nor for
  Master of Storms, Deepening Study and Stormcaller (they only unlock spells). Class icon: Wolf
  Howl. Announced subclasses: Circle of Cinder & Ash, Circle of Venom & Web.
- **Zephyr, Extra Subclasses**: not started.

## Spells

- **Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind**: in the new style and aligned with both changelogs
  (Echoburst replaces Fly, Omenrime replaces Cryosleep).
- **Radiant**: in the new style and aligned with both changelogs (Celestial Legion, a new
  changelog spell at tier 7; Shield of Justice moves to tier 4, Condemn to tier 3).
  Lifebinding Spirit, reserved for the Shepherd, is in `game/class/shepherd/`.
- **Necrotic**: in the new style and aligned with both changelogs (Vigor Mortis, new at tier
  4, replaces Greater Shadow, which became a Shadowmancer ability). The spells reserved for
  the Shadowmancer are in `game/class/shadowmancer/`. School icon: Skull Bolt.
- **Utility**: the book's 18 spells (3 per school) in each school's `utility/` folder, aligned
  with the changelogs (Ice Disk, Wintry Scrying, Wind Whisper, Spark Buddy). Casting time as
  the CAST stat.

## Basic actions (`game/actions/`)

- 3 cards every hero gets, type "Ability · Combat", Crossed Swords icon: *Basic Actions*
  (Move, Attack, Hide; Hide with no roll, house rule), *Grapple* (Grab, Escape: a STR or DEX
  check against 10 + the other creature's higher of the two, house rule; Assess removed from
  the game), *Triggered Actions* (Defend, Interpose, Opportunity Attack; Help removed, house rule). Up to the
  October 2, 2026 changelog. The Combat page doesn't repeat these actions, it refers to the
  cards.

## Items (`game/items/`)

- **Weapons**: DONE, 16 melee and 7 ranged, one card each (DMG, REACH or RNGE, SLOT; no

  becomes the Greatspear, a new Spear whose stats are a guess). Weapon rules (proficiency,
  swapping, dual wielding, unarmed strikes, improvised weapons) are on the Combat page,
  inventory slots on the Adventure page.

  hazards are deliberately left out.
