# Card format — Nimblium

The card types, their fields and how they are rendered. For the text itself, see
`writing_rules.txt`; for creatures, `creature_rules.txt`.

## Card types (`templates/`)

- `creat_ability.typ` → **Ability** cards
- `creat_spell.typ` → **Spell** cards
- `creat_creature.typ` → **Creature** cards
- `creat_mechanic.typ` → **Mechanic** cards
- `creat_item.typ` → **Item** cards (weapons; armor, shields and gear to come)

### Shared layout (`creat_frame.typ`)

Every type goes through `creat_card(name, types, body, stats:, icon:)`, which renders the
front and the back side by side (5 mm apart). A clean design, black and white, little ink:

- Front: white frame with a black border, "HUD" corners, the name in spaced capitals + the
  types in small print (no rule; a list that is too long wraps, only after a "·", and what
  follows moves down accordingly), then the stat row (creatures and spells), a fixed 1.5 mm
  under the types: a small short label (4 letters at most, except `REACH`) above each value,
  thin vertical lines between the boxes. It stays on one line as long as its values fit at
  8pt minimum; otherwise it goes on two lines, each centered, separated by a thin horizontal
  line (60 % of the widest line). The rules text, left-aligned, is centered vertically in the
  space left under the stat row. If it overflows, the stats → text gap, the gap between the
  two stat lines and the gaps between blocks tighten together, then the text shrinks down to
  6pt. The paragraphs of one block (the gray reminders, the paragraphs of an effect) are
  spaced at 60 % of the gap between blocks, so they tighten with it.
- Back: name + types centered, under a large family icon in gray `#999999` (game-icons.net
  CC BY 3.0, or Bootstrap Icons MIT; see `art/icons/CREDITS.md`). The icon comes from the
  card's types: `family_icons.typ` maps a type to a file in `art/icons/` (today Goblin,
  Undead, the Fire, Ice, Lightning, Wind, Radiant and Necrotic schools, the Berserker, Cheat,
  Commander, Hunter, Mage, Oathsworn, Shepherd, Shadowmancer, Songweaver and Stormshifter classes, and Combat for the
  basic actions); if several types match, the most specific one (the last) wins. With no
  known type, there is no icon and the name is centered. The same icon appears in small (6 mm)
  at the bottom center of the front, straddling the line of the bottom corners, raised 1 mm
  for the eye; the rules text then stops above it.
- An `icon` field in the card data replaces the family icon on the back (the front keeps the
  family icon): `icon: "x.svg"` is looked up in `art/icons/`, a path starting with `/` starts
  from the project root. SVGs are turned gray, other images (PNG) stay as they are. Nothing is
  linked automatically by file name. To add a family: `art.txt`, "Family icons" section.
- Abilities and spells: dark capsules (ACTIVE, PASSIVE, CANTRIP, UPCAST…) + outlined capsules
  (costs, limits), aligned left, with the text right below.
- Creatures: section bars `── PASSIVE ──` (no capsules), shown only if the card has several
  sections.

### Ability (`creat_ability.typ`)

`blocks` is a list of `(kind, [cost], [limitation], effect)`.

- `kind`: `"active"` (costs an action or a resource, triggered by the player's choice),
  `"passive"` (automatic or triggered effect, no action spent to activate it), `"narrative"`
  (advantage on checks / flavor / role-play — e.g. Forager, Leave No Trace, Thieves'
  Cant, Keen Sight).
- `name` (optional): a bold name above the capsules, for a card that gathers several named
  actions (the basic action cards, Dragon Form).
- `cost`: a tuple of tags shown as badges, e.g. `(actions(1),)`, `(actions(2), toth_charges(1))`.
- `tags` (optional): outlined capsules after the cost, e.g. `("AoE",)` (Whirlwind).
- `limitation`: a tag shown as a badge, e.g. `per_turn(1)`, `per_encounter(1)`,
  `per_safe_rest(WIL)`, `per_round(2)`.
- Blocks are sorted: narrative first, then passive, then active (creatures too).
- Level progression and multiple clauses: see `writing_rules.txt` §8.
- `type`: `("Ability", "<Class>")` for a base ability, with a 3rd item for a subgroup
  (subclass or pool of choices), e.g. `("Ability","Cheat","Underhanded")`,
  `("Ability","Cheat","Scoundrel")`, `("Ability","Hunter",thrill_of_the_hunt)`,
  `("Ability","Hunter","Shadowpath")`, `("Ability","Shepherd","Sacred Grace")`. The basic
  actions every hero has are `("Ability","Combat")` (`game/actions/`).

### Item (`creat_item.typ`)

Weapons today (`game/items/weapons/melee/`, `game/items/weapons/ranged/`), one card per item,
no cost. Fields:

- `type`: `("Item", "Weapon", "Melee" | "Ranged", <category>)`, e.g.
  `("Item","Weapon","Melee","Blade")`. The category describes the weapon (there is no
  weapon proficiency; abilities may name it): Blade (daggers, sickle, swords, rapier, glaive), Axe, Hammer (mace, hammers),
  Staff, Polearm (spears), Bow, Crossbow, Sling, Thrown (javelins, throwing hammers).
- `family_icon`: the icon of its kind, on both faces (`dagger.svg`, `sword.svg`,
  `throwing_axe.svg`, `bow.svg`...), since a category such as Blade holds several kinds.
- The damage type is the first block: `weapon_damage("Piercing")`.
- Stat row, in this order: `damage` (DMG, e.g. `[1d8+#STR]`), `reach` (REACH, every melee
  weapon, even 1), `range` (RNGE: ranged weapons, and the range of a thrown weapon), `slots`
  (SLOT, always last: inventory slots, Adventure page).
- `blocks`: the weapon's properties, as named ability blocks. The shared ones are in
  `game/items/weapons/weapon_properties.typ`: `weapon_properties.one_handed`, `.two_handed`, `.light`,
  `.thrown`, `.vicious`, and the functions `weapon_two_handed_unless(n)`, `weapon_heavy(n)`
  (the book's "Req. n STR"), `weapon_stack(n)`, `weapon_load(n)` (an active block that costs
  n actions). The text is written in full on each card: no reminder.
- A two-handed weapon takes 2 slots, everything else 1 (a stack of javelins is 1).
- Armor and the shield (`game/items/armor/`): `type: ("Item", "Armor", "Cloth" | "Leather" |
  "Mail" | "Plate")` or `("Item", "Shield")`, `defense` (DEF: the level 1 bonus, `[+6]`),
  `slots` (2 for armor, 1 for the shield), `high_Levels: armor_levels((9, 12, 15))` (the
  bonus at levels 5, 10, 15). Blocks from `game/items/armor/armor_properties.typ`:
  `armor_bonus` or `shield_bonus` (unnamed), then `armor_max_dex(n)` (Mail), `armor_no_dex`
  (Plate), `armor_heavy(n)`; the shield also has `weapon_properties.one_handed`.

### Mechanic (`creat_mechanic.typ`)

Used to gather in one place a rule or a resource that spans several levels or the whole
class, rather than making one ability card per level. A `description` field (not
`blocks`/`effect`). Precedents: `game/class/hunter/thrill_of_the_hunt_charge.typ` (rules for
gaining TotH charges), `game/class/shepherd/shepherd_mana_pool.typ` (mana pool formula +
spell tiers unlocked by level, with the same `*Level X:*` style as `sneak_attack.typ`).

### Spell (`creat_spell.typ`)

Available fields: `cost`, `casting_time` (casting time in minutes, CAST stat, e.g.
`"1 min"`), `concentration`, `range`, `reach`, `area`, `damage`, `effect`, `high_Levels`,
`upcast`, `flavor`, `reminder`. `type` is always (type, school, tier, tags...), e.g.
`("Spell","Fire","Cantrip")`, `("Spell","Lightning","Tier 3","AoE")`. Utility spells put
`"Utility"` in place of the tier: `("Spell","Ice","Utility")` (in their school's `utility/`
folder, e.g. `game/spells/ice/utility/`, with their school's icon).

Rendering (no `Cost:` or `Effect:` label):

- The subtitle shows the whole `type`: type, school, tier and tags, e.g.
  `SPELL · FIRE · TIER 5 · AOE` or `SPELL · LIGHTNING · CANTRIP`.
- Everything that describes the whole spell goes in the stat row, in this order: `ACT`
  (cost in actions), `MANA`, `CONC` (concentration), then `DMG`, `RNGE` (range), `REACH`,
  `AREA`. If the row goes on two lines, the break falls between casting (ACT, MANA, CONC) and
  the effect (the rest).
- Any other cost stays as a capsule (`badges.typ`) above the effect.
- `damage` only holds the value ("3d8+4"): any detail goes in `effect`.
- `reach`: a number ("4") or a shape ("Line 12", "Cone 4"), shown under REACH.
- `concentration` only holds the duration ("1 min").
- `high_Levels` and `upcast`: a dark capsule, then the text on the next line.
- `upcast` can be a list of options (`upcast: ([...], [...])`): one UPCAST capsule per
  option, and the caster picks one for each tier.
- `flavor`: flavor text in italics, under the rules and before the reminders.

### Creature (`creat_creature.typ`)

Format (`creature_rules.txt`): `blocks` like hero abilities, plus a `name` per block (`name`,
`kind`, `cost`, `limitation`, `effect`) and, for attacks, `attacks`, `reach`, `range` and
`damage`, shown as capsules on the cost line; `actions` (ACT stat, shown first and only if
> 1), `reminder`, `hit_points`, `defense`, `speed`, `level`, `str`, `dex`, `int`, `wil` (shown
only when not 0), `icon`. The shared blocks are in
`game/creatures/passive_abilities.typ`: `passive_abilities.undying` and
`passive_abilities.minion`. One creature = one file = one card by default (a text that is too
long overflows, so that it shows). The `split: true` option allows the automatic split into
two cards "1/2" and "2/2" (see `creature_rules.txt`); the exported PDF then shows both, one
under the other. No card shared by legendary creatures (tried, then removed: it added
nothing): Bloodied and Last Stand are written in full on each legendary creature, and its
saves become stats. Any
other key is silently ignored.

## Class companions: separate the spell from the creature

When a class ability summons a companion (e.g. the Shepherd's Lifebinding Spirit), split it
into two cards:
- The **spell** (`creat_spell`) only says "summon your Companion... lasts until X" and doesn't
  repeat the stats or attacks.
- The **creature** (`creat_creature`) documents every detail (attacks, heal, reach, scaling
  die, etc.) in `passive`/`active` sections.

See `game/class/shepherd/lifebinding_spirit.typ` + `game/class/shepherd/spirit_companion.typ`.
Same principle for a spell that grants new actions (a transformation): the spell describes the
effect and says "You gain the X abilities", and a separate `creat_ability` card holds the
actions (each action's name in the block's `name` field). See `game/spells/fire/dragonform.typ`
+ `game/spells/fire/dragon_form.typ`. Spells have no `blocks` field.
