# House rules — Nimblium

Every place where Nimblium deliberately departs from Nimble (books and changelogs). These are
the user's decisions: never "fix" them from the book or a changelog.

Each line says where the rule applies. The details are in the `house-rule:` note of the file
it cites; `python3 tools/review.py --rules` lists them all. When a `house-rule:` note is added
to a card, add or complete a line here.

## Whole game

- **No change of choices on a rest.** Every class in the book has an ability that lets you
  redo your class choices during a Safe Rest (Supplicate, Remember the Wild, Serve, Be Wild,
  Focus, "you may choose different X options"...). It is not used: too strong, and it slows
  the game down. Choices only change when you level up. No card, and no Retrain activity
  during downtime.
- **No Reactions.** A reaction is an action with a "When..." trigger, a cost of 1 action and a
  limit of 1/round. It also works on your own turn. (`writing_rules.txt` §4,
  `game/rules/combat.typ`, `game/actions/triggered_actions.typ`)
- **Opportunity Attacks.** Only heroes make them, unless a creature's card says otherwise.
  (`game/actions/triggered_actions.typ`)
- **Hide with no roll.** 1 action with Cover is enough to hide (Nimble: Stealth DC 10, Full
  Cover succeeds automatically). (`game/keywords.typ`, `game/actions/basic_actions.typ`)
- **Combat rules are objective.** No combat rule depends on the GM's judgment of a
  description. What is narrative (asking a question, helping an ally, a simple task) is
  role-play, handled by the GM without a rule.
- **No Help.** Removed from the triggered actions for that reason (Nimble: 1 action, an ally
  rerolls if you explain how you help). (`game/actions/triggered_actions.typ`)
- **No Assess.** Asking the GM a question is role-play. Its two combat effects (Create an
  Opening, Anticipate Danger) live on *Wild Instinct* and *Quick Read*; *Exploit Weakness*
  becomes an action of its own. (`game/actions/grapple.typ`)
- **Grappling.** To grab (Grab) and to break free (Escape), the creature that acts makes a STR
  or DEX check against 10 + the higher of the other creature's two stats (changelog: Might or
  Finesse check, DC 10; book: the target makes a save). Creatures that grab simply say so
  ("the target is Grappled"). (`game/actions/grapple.typ`)
- **Values missing from creatures.** When a rule needs a value a creature's card doesn't give
  (STR, DEX, a skill...), the GM decides it. (`game/rules/combat.typ`, Creatures section)
- **No card for simple tasks** (opening a door, dropping an item): the GM handles them.
  (`game/actions/basic_actions.typ`)
- **Cover and Full Cover** only affect ranged attacks, and are worded with "line of attack".
  (`game/keywords.typ`)

## Creatures

- **Creature actions.** Unlike Nimble (one thing per turn, "OR" / "Then"), creatures have
  actions like heroes (1 by default, `ACT` stat if more) and their abilities are blocks with
  capsules. Moving is free. (`creature_rules.txt`)
- **Monster Defense** (from the October 2, 2026 changelog). Monster armor becomes their
  Defense, a number that reduces the damage they take: Medium = DEF 10, Heavy = DEF 20.
  `defense: 10` field on the card, `DEF` box. Crits ignore it. Hero cards say "ignoring
  #defense". "Armor" only remains for worn equipment.
- **Goblins.** Haha, Didn't Hurt! (the changelog's family ability) and the traps and hazards
  of Goblins v1.1 are left out. (`game/creatures/goblin/mod.typ`)

## Classes

- **Cheat.**
  - *Sunder Armor (Medium)* and *(Heavy)* stay two abilities that lower Defense by 10 and 20,
    to match monster Defense (the changelog merges them).
  - *Exploit Weakness*: 1 action, no roll and no limit (book: an opposed INT check).
  - *Quick Read*: free, 1/encounter, a creature's Primary Dice against you get −1 this round
    (book: advantage on Assess and on an Examination check).
- **Commander.**
  - *Shield Expert*: the free Defend is limited to 1/round (decided on 2026-11-21).
  - *Survey the Battlefield*: 1 more use of *Coordinated Strike!* per encounter.
- **Hunter.**
  - *No Escape*: a 2/round limit, not in the book.
  - *Wild Instinct*: free, 1/round, with no charges: +1 to the next Primary Die against a
    creature, or −1 to the Primary Dice against you, with no roll (book: Assess with
    advantage).
- **Shepherd.** The companion stays in its house version, very different from the official
  game: a separate creature with an HP pool (Tier × 10) that acts in contact. The October 2,
  2026 changelog changes to the companion are not applied, except two: heals limited to
  WIL/Safe Rest, and *Vengeful Spirit* as a single burst. The adapted cards (*Lifebinding
  Spirit*, *Assist Me, My Friend*, *Empowered Companion*, *Hasty Companion*, *Vengeful
  Spirit*...) each say so. (`game/class/shepherd/mod.typ`)

## Spells

- **Creeping Death**: the chain repeats at most 4 times, plus 1 per upcast (decided on
  2026-11-21; the book sets no limit).
