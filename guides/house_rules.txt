# House rules — Nimblium

Every place where Nimblium deliberately departs from Nimble (books and changelogs). These are
the user's decisions: never "fix" them from the book or a changelog.

Each line says where the rule applies. The details are in the `house-rule:` note of the file
it cites; `python3 tools/review.py --rules` lists them all. When a `house-rule:` note is added
to a card, add or complete a line here.

## Whole game

- **No change of choices on a rest.** Every class in the book has an ability that lets you
  redo your class choices during a Safe Rest (Supplicate, Remember the Wild, Serve, Be Wild,
  Focus, Perform!, Jack of All Trades, Be Wild, "you may choose different X options"...). It is not used: too strong, and it slows
  the game down. Choices never change, not even when you level up (changelog: you may change
  them on a level up): a new level only adds new choices. No card, and no Retrain activity
  during downtime.
- **No maximum of 3 actions.** Effects that give actions can take you above 3. At the end of
  your turn, the actions you have left are lost and you get exactly 3 (book: up to 3 actions,
  and you get back up to 3). (`game/rules/combat.typ`, Actions)
- **No Reactions.** A reaction is an action with a "When..." trigger, a cost of 1 action and a
  limit of 1/round. It also works on your own turn. (`writing_rules.txt` §4,
  `game/rules/combat.typ`, `game/actions/triggered_actions.typ`)
- **A check beats the DC.** A check succeeds when its total is higher than the DC, not equal
  to it (user ruling 2026-11-28; book: equal or higher): against DC 10 with no bonus, it is
  50%. (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **No saves, one kind of check.** A check is 1d20 + the stat it names (plus the skill, if it names one), against the
  same DCs; there is no separate "skill check" and "stat check". An effect that the book
  resolves with a save calls for a check by the affected creature, against the DC the card states (usually DC 10+KEY for an effect
  a hero creates). Cards that said "skill checks" now say "checks", including the *Cheat* card ("change the
  result of one of your checks to 10+INT"), which now also covers stat checks. Classes have no advantaged or disadvantaged saves, and the class sheets have no
  Saves line. There is no "half on save" rule: each card says what a success does. (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **Advantage: remove the dice of your choice** (user ruling 2026-11-28). With advantage, you
  roll the extra dice and remove that many dice of your choice, so a die at its maximum can
  become your Primary Die (book: remove the lowest). Disadvantage still removes the highest,
  left to right when they tie. (`game/keywords.typ`)
- **Opportunity Attacks.** Only heroes make them, unless a creature's card says otherwise. One
  triggers when a creature leaves your Reach, and the attack has no disadvantage (user ruling
  2026-11-28; book: an adjacent creature willingly moving away, attacked with disadvantage).
  (`game/actions/triggered_actions.typ`)
- **Long range.** A ranged attack reaches up to twice its Range, with 1 disadvantage beyond its
  Range (user ruling 2026-11-28; book: 1 disadvantage for each 2 more spaces, up to 3 times).
  (`game/rules/combat.typ`)
- **Concentration ends on a critical hit**, with no roll (user ruling 2026-11-28; book: a DC 10
  STR check). (`game/keywords.typ`)
- **Hide with no roll.** 1 action with Cover is enough to hide (Nimble: Stealth DC 10, Full
  Cover succeeds automatically). (`game/keywords.typ`, `game/actions/basic_actions.typ`)
- **Combat rules are objective.** No combat rule depends on the GM's judgment of a
  description. What is narrative (asking a question, helping an ally, a simple task) is
  role-play, handled by the GM without a rule.
- **No Help.** Removed from the triggered actions for that reason (Nimble: 1 action, an ally
  rerolls if you explain how you help). (`game/actions/triggered_actions.typ`)
- **No Assess.** Asking the GM a question is role-play. Its two combat effects (Create an
  Opening, Anticipate Danger) live on *Wild Instinct* and *Quick Read*; *Exploit Weakness*
  becomes an action of its own. (`game/actions/grapple.typ`)
- **Grappling** (simplified, user ruling 2026-11-28). To grab (Grab, 1 action, a free hand, a
  creature within your Reach, your size or smaller) and to break free (Escape, 1 action), the
  creature that acts makes a check adding its STR and DEX, against DC 10 plus the other
  creature's STR and DEX (changelog: Might or Finesse check, DC 10; book: the target makes a
  save). Grappled only concerns the grabbed creature: it can't move and attacks against it have
  advantage. The grapple ends when the grappler lets go, moves away or is Incapacitated (book:
  the grappler moves at half speed and is attacked with advantage; it ends on a crit or a
  Knockback; grabbing a bigger creature makes you ride it). Creatures that grab simply say so
  ("the target is Grappled"). (`game/conditions.typ`, `game/actions/grapple.typ`)
- **Creature stats.** A creature's STR, DEX, INT and WIL are 0 unless its card shows them,
  and so are its skills (a check adds the stat and the skill, as for heroes). Nimble's monster saves become stats: each "+" is +3,
  each "−" is −3. (`game/rules/combat.typ`, Adversaries section; `creature_rules.txt` §4)
- **Knockback is the only forced movement.** The size rule (Small, Tiny and Flying ×2, Large
  half, Huge and Gargantuan not at all) is part of the Knockback keyword; other cards that move
  a creature do exactly what they say. A creature stopped by an obstacle and the creature it
  hits each take the pusher's LVL+1 per space not moved (book: 1d6 per space,
  shared), so the damage keeps pace with HP. Rushed attacks only add disadvantage to attack rolls
  (the book also gave the target advantage on its save). (`game/keywords.typ`)
- **No "who can hear you".** A range the book gives as "who can hear you" or "within
  hearing" becomes a number of spaces (18 by default), so that no GM ruling is needed.
- **Every effect on an ally states its distance.** When the book gives none, the card says
  "within 18 spaces". There is no default distance rule: the card always says it.
- **Round up.** When a rule divides a number, the result is rounded up.
  (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **No card for simple tasks** (opening a door, dropping an item): the GM handles them.
  (`game/actions/basic_actions.typ`)
- **Max HP = 10 + LVL × Vitality** (class value + STR), with
  no roll and no starting HP per class. Class sheets show "d8, +5 HP per level" (book: a
  starting HP per class, then roll the Hit Die with advantage or take the fixed value).
  (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **Cover and Full Cover** only affect ranged attacks, and are worded with "line of attack".
  (`game/keywords.typ`)

## Creatures

- **Creature actions.** Unlike Nimble (one thing per turn, "OR" / "Then"), creatures have
  actions like heroes (1 by default, `ACT` stat if more) and their abilities are blocks with
  capsules. Moving is free. (`creature_rules.txt`)
- **Monster Defense** (from the October 2, 2026 changelog). Monster armor becomes their
  Defense, a number that reduces the damage they take: Medium = DEF 10, Heavy = DEF 20.
  `defense: 10` field on the card, `DEF` box. Crits ignore it. Hero cards say "ignoring
  #defense". "Armor" only remains for worn equipment.
- **No legendary creatures** (user ruling 2026-11-28). A boss is a creature like the others,
  with a name, often more actions (ACT) and a higher level, and it acts on the creatures' turn
  (book: a legendary monster takes a turn after each hero's turn). Krogg and Vermis &
  Skittersnack have ACT 3 (a cautious start) and 3 times their book level, so a boss alone is
  worth the levels of 3 heroes. (`game/rules/combat.typ`, `creature_rules.txt` §1)
- **Goblins.** Haha, Didn't Hurt! (the changelog's family ability) and the traps and hazards
  of Goblins v1.1 are left out. (`game/creatures/goblin/mod.typ`)

## Classes

- **Cheat.**
  - *Sunder Armor (Medium)* and *(Heavy)* stay two abilities that lower Defense by 10 and 20,
    to match monster Defense (the changelog merges them).
  - *Exploit Weakness*: 1 action, no roll and no limit (book: an opposed INT check).
  - *Quick Read*: free, 1/encounter, a creature's Primary Dice against you get −1 this round
    (book: advantage on Assess and on an Examination check).
- **Commander.**
  - *Shield Expert*: the free Defend is limited to 1/round (decided on 2026-11-21).
  - *Survey the Battlefield*: 1 more use of *Coordinated Strike!* per encounter.
- **Hunter.**
  - *No Escape*: a 2/round limit, not in the book.
  - *Wild Instinct*: free, 1/round, with no charges: +1 to the next Primary Die against a
    creature, or −1 to the Primary Dice against you, with no roll (book: Assess with
    advantage).
- **Songweaver.** The friends of A "People" Person are creatures that follow the Songweaver
  instead of being summoned with a song. Stompy is a companion like the Shepherd's: 5×LVL HP,
  a 1d6+LVL Slam, and a Stomp (LVL+10 to each creature in a 6-space line) once per Safe Rest,
  with no Influence check. The others have LVL+5 HP and no attack: Gran Gran bakes pastries,
  Mal is a flying spy (DEX +3, INT +2, +5 Stealth and Perception), and Linos is a Huge flying
  mount with 5×LVL HP and speed 12. (`game/class/songweaver/friends/`)
- **Stormshifter.**
  - *Beast of Nightmares* is a Swarm and can be targeted like any creature (book: it can't be
    targeted until it becomes "conspicuous").
  - *Attuned to Nature* adds LVL to a Naturecraft check (book: any check "related to nature
    or weather").
- **Shepherd.** The companion stays in its house version, very different from the official
  game: a separate creature with an HP pool (Tier × 10) that acts in contact. The October 2,
  2026 changelog changes to the companion are not applied, except two: heals limited to
  WIL/Safe Rest, and *Vengeful Spirit* as a single burst. The adapted cards (*Lifebinding
  Spirit*, *Assist Me, My Friend*, *Empowered Companion*, *Hasty Companion*, *Vengeful
  Spirit*...) each say so. (`game/class/shepherd/mod.typ`)

## Spells

- **Creeping Death**: the chain repeats at most 4 times, plus 1 per upcast (decided on
  2026-11-21; the book sets no limit).

## Items

- **No improvised weapon rule** (user ruling 2026-11-28): the GM decides what a chair or a rock
  does (book: the GM picks the die). Customizing weapons is left to the GM too.
- **Spear.** The 2026-10-02 changelog adds a Spear without its stats: 1d8+STR, Reach 2,
  two-handed or one-handed with 2 STR, a guess between the Longsword and the Greatspear.
  (`game/items/weapons/melee/spear.typ`)
- **Weapon requirements.** The book's "Req. n STR" on a weapon becomes Heavy: you can't
  attack with it below n STR (the book gives no penalty). (`weapon_properties.typ`)
- **No prices on items**: the cards show no cost. (`game/items/`)
- **No weapon proficiency.** Any hero can use any weapon at full power, and class sheets list
  no weapons (book: a weapon without proficiency can't crit). Unarmed strikes can crit like
  any attack (user ruling 2026-11-28). Armor proficiency stays. (`game/rules/combat.typ`)
- **Dual wielding**: with a Light weapon in each hand, your first attack each round has
  advantage (book: one attack per round has advantage, roll both weapons' damage and keep
  either, and 2 or 3 STR lift the Light requirement). (`game/rules/combat.typ`, Weapons)
- **Changing what you hold** costs 1 action; dropping an item is free (book: swap a weapon
  you are proficient with for free, 1/round). Thrown weapons stay in hand while they have
  charges. (`game/rules/combat.typ`, Hands)
- **Hands.** A hero has 2 hands. Each item card says how many hands it takes (One-Handed,
  Two-Handed); a Two-Handed weapon takes both hands, even when you don't attack with it (book:
  it can be held in 1 hand). (`game/rules/combat.typ`, Hands)
- **Armor.** One card per category (Cloth, Leather, Mail, Plate) and one Shield, whose bonus
  grows with your level: the book's 4 tiers (bought with gold) become levels 1, 5, 10 and 15.
  Defense is DEX + armor + shield (Mail: DEX adds at most 2; Plate: DEX doesn't add). Mail
  needs 2 STR and Plate 3 (Heavy: you can't wear it below); the other requirements are
  dropped. Armor takes 2 slots, worn or not. You wear 1 armor at a time and only 1 shield adds to
  Defense (written on the cards). (`game/items/armor/`)
- **Armor proficiency**: class sheets list every armor the class is proficient with, lighter
  ones included (book: the heaviest only), and you can't wear or hold the others (book: Defend
  costs 1 more action without proficiency). The Berserker and the Zephyr wear none, as in the
  book. (`game/rules/combat.typ`)
- **Charges.** Potions and wands have charges (CHRG) that all come back after a Safe Rest
  (book: bought one by one, used up); there is no Consumable item type. Slots limit how many you carry. Scrolls merge into
  wands. (`game/rules/adventure.typ`, Items)
- **Item level.** An item may show a LVL: you can't use it below that level. A wand's LVL is
  twice its spell's tier (1 for a cantrip). (`game/rules/adventure.typ`, Items)
- **Thrown weapons** have charges (a stack of javelins is 4); each throw costs 1, and you can
  pick them up and regain them when combat ends (book: once thrown, you no longer have it). Javelins and Throwing Hammers take 2 slots.
  (`weapon_properties.typ`)
- **Healing Potion**: one card, 1 charge, 2 slots; it heals 2, 3, 4 then 5 × Vitality at
  levels 1, 5, 10, 15 (book: 2d4+4, 3d6+6, 4d8+8, three potions bought separately).
  (`game/items/gear/healing_potion.typ`)
- **Wand**: one blank card the GM fills in (spell, level = 2 × tier or 1 for a cantrip,
  charges 3/2/1 by tier, 2 slots). Anyone can use it, with no proficiency or Arcana check; spell scrolls
  are wands with 1 charge. (`game/items/gear/wand.typ`)
- **Gear.** Everyday items have no card of their own: the GM writes them on a blank Small,
  Bulky or Heavy Gear card (1, 2, 3 slots). Torch and lantern are one Torch card that doesn't
  burn out. (`game/items/gear/`)
- **No spellcasting focus.** Casting a spell needs a free hand (book: a free hand or a held
  focus, which is never defined). *Aura of Refuge* makes a held shield count as a free hand
  for casting (book: shields count as your focus). (`game/rules/magic.typ`)
- **Magic items.** You carry at most 2 magic items, 3 from level 5, 4 from level 10, 5 from
  level 15 (book: no limit), as the box at the top of each class sheet says; wands are not
  magic items. Rarity becomes LVL: Uncommon 1, Rare 5, Very
  Rare 10, Legendary 15. Items whose power varies with rarity (Many Hands, Animosity,
  Slaying) grow with your level instead. Weapon enchantments are cards put with a weapon card.
  Saves become checks, reactions become "When..." actions, recharges become charges back after
  a Safe Rest. No item is ever destroyed by its own use (Gem of Escape, Phoenix Helm): it
  has a charge instead. Purely narrative magic items have no card: the GM writes them on a
  blank Gear card. (`game/items/magic/`, `game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **Zephyr.** Martial Training (level 1) carries its unarmed proficiency: its fists count as
  dual wielded (every hero's unarmed strikes can crit). The Burst of Speed maneuvers cost their usual
  action plus 1 Burst (book: free, 1/turn; the changelog removes the limit and gives Swift
  Fists, which makes Galeforce Strike cost 0 actions in one case). Iron Defense adds STR to
  Defense (Defense = DEX + armor). (`game/class/zephyr/`)
- **Supernatural Speed** (Zephyr): spend X Mom charges when an attack hits you to take X less
  damage (book: lower the attacker's Primary Die by 1 per Momentum). (`game/class/zephyr/supernatural_speed.typ`)
- **Redeem** (Radiant, Tier 9): cast during a Safe Rest, once per level, with no diamond and no
  "only once per creature" limit (book: 24 hours, a 10,000 gp diamond, within 1 mile; written 2 km).
  (`game/spells/radiant/redeem.typ`)
- **Stat and skill points** (user ruling 2026-11-28). Stats and skills start at 0 and have no
  maximum (book: stats at most +5, skills at most +10). Raising a stat or a skill by 1 costs as
  many points as its new value, so high values grow slowly. A hero has 5 stat points and
  10 skill points at level 1, then gains 1 stat point and 2 skill points at every level. Unspent points are kept, and points never move. A check with a skill adds the stat
  and the skill (book: the skill already includes its stat). No stat arrays, no Secondary
  Stats, no stat increases at set levels on the class sheets (a box above their level
  table gives what every level brings: 10+Vitality HP, 5 stat points, 10 skill points and 2
  magic items at level 1, then +Vitality HP, +1, +2, and +1 magic item at levels 5, 10, 15). Key Stats stay: they say which stat is KEY.
  (`game/rules/adventure.typ`, `game/hero_sheet.typ`)
- **No Hit Dice, no Field Rest.** Vitality (the class's value + STR; the classes with
  STR as a Key Stat have 1 less than their old Hit Die value) replaces the Hit Die, gives the
  HP per level and counts heals. The Safe Rest is the only rest. Cards that spent Hit
  Dice were rewritten (Healing Potion, I Can Do This ALL DAY!, Infuse Strength, Enduring Soul,
  Field Medic, Song of Rest, Impressive Form, Fiendish Boon, Mountainous Tenacity, Warm
  Pastries). (`game/rules/adventure.typ`, `game/rules/level_up.typ`)
- **No healing farmed from short fights** (user ruling 2026-11-27): anything that restores HP
  outside a cost is limited per Rest, never per encounter. Song of Rest is 2/Rest (book: Hit Dice
  bonus), Hold the Line 1/Rest (book: 1/encounter), and the ancestries' heals too (see Ancestries).
  Temporary HP are lost when combat ends (before: they lasted until a Rest), so a lucky roll on
  Enduring Soul can't be carried to the next fight. (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **Rest.** The book's Safe Rest is simply the Rest: a night of sleep, about 8 hours, when the GM
  says the heroes can rest, with no need for a safe place. Cards read it through `#safe_rest`
  and `per_safe_rest(n)` ("1/Rest"). Guides may still say Safe Rest: it is the same thing.
- **Dying**: whenever a Dying creature would take damage, it gains 1 Wound instead, or 2 on a
  critical hit; no STR check when it attacks (book: a STR check on each attack, and 2 Wounds per
  hit, 3 on a crit). Death at 6 Wounds. A Safe Rest heals all your Wounds (user ruling
  2026-11-28; book: 1 per Safe Rest). Unstoppable Force, Enduring Rage and Unyielding Resolve
  were adjusted. (`game/conditions.typ`)
- **No languages** for now: the Languages rule, Thieves' Cant (Cheat) and Devoted Acolyte
  (Shadowmancer) are removed, and ancestries give no language. (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **Ancestries**: all 24 of the Core Rules, renamed "X-folk" (Bunbun is Rabbit-folk, Kobold is
  Lizard-folk, Celestial is Angel-folk...), with no Common/Exotic tiers and no language. Shape-folk
  (Changeling) is dropped. Balance pass (2026-11-27): one main trait per ancestry, passives use
  fixed numbers (Dwarf-folk +2 Vitality, Giant-folk +1 STR; Turtle-folk +KEY Defense), and
  limited actives work 1/encounter for big effects, 2/encounter for small reactive ones; anything that
  restores HP is per Rest, so short fights can't be farmed (Angel-folk's Healing Light and Lizard-folk's
  Regrowth 2/Rest; Orc-folk's Relentless 1/Rest, drops you to 3×KEY HP); KEY only scales
  their effect. Bull-folk charges (1/encounter): after 4 spaces of movement, a melee hit
  deals LVL+KEY more damage and gives Knockback 2. Identity pass (2026-11-27): a bonus belongs to one ancestry
  only (Vitality to Dwarf-folk, Defense to Turtle-folk, Initiative to Human-folk, checks to
  Small-folk and Human-folk, a free move to Rabbit-folk); Angel-folk heals, Fiend-folk
  frightens, Goblin-folk swaps with an ally, Rat-folk moves through larger creatures,
  Construct-folk overdrives (1 Wound for 2 actions), Lizard-folk heals itself (2×(LVL+KEY) HP,
  2/Rest). Every block of an ancestry card has a name. (`game/ancestries/`)
- **Backgrounds** (user ruling 2026-11-28): not the book's backgrounds. A background is where
  you grew up, your social origin: Noble, Fallen, Soldier, Guard, Bureaucrat, Acolyte, Street
  Kid, Outlaw, Outlander, Laborer, Artisan, Mechanic, Merchant, Scholar, Nomad, Artist, Outcast,
  Captive, Uncanny. Each card has a clear name, a narrative block with no name (where you grew up, what you
  did, how you see the world: "To you, the world...") and one small effect tied to that origin,
  mostly for combat: Noble +1 magic item, Fallen Grudge, Soldier +KEY damage next to an ally
  and Outcast +KEY damage with no ally within 6 spaces, Guard a free Interpose, Bureaucrat
  advantage on Influence with officials, Acolyte Last Rites (1/Rest), Street Kid Hide without
  Cover, Outlaw a free move (like Rabbit-folk's), Outlander ignores Difficult Terrain, Laborer
  +4 slots, Artisan ignores Defense once, Mechanic regains 1 charge (1/Rest), Merchant Quick
  Hands, Scholar learns resistances, Nomad +1 speed, Artist Showstopper, Captive temp HP when
  Bloodied, Uncanny forces a reroll of the Primary Die. (`game/backgrounds/`)
- **Chaos Table** is public, on the Magic page (book: the GM's secret). The Control Table stays a
  card of the Invoker of Control. (`game/rules/magic.typ`)
- **No default DC**: each card states the DC of the checks it calls for (usually DC 10+KEY); the
  rules pages give no default. (`game/rules/adventure.typ`, `game/rules/magic.typ`)
- **Success at a cost**: when a check fails, the GM chooses: a failure, or a success at a cost.
  (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
- **No Boons** (2026-11-28): the book's Minor, Major and Epic Boons are not used, and the
  level 19 Epic Boon is removed from every class sheet: that level gives nothing. (`guides/todo.txt`)
- **No falling rule**: the GM decides what a fall does. (`game/rules/adventure.typ`)
